1.1 - Introducing Ava (Dialogue)

Alabama

Holchifaya Ava.

Avaya aathomma imoolafan wiikahchi.

Himaakaya albaama inna!iilkan sobaylihchi.

Mikkok albaamo innaa!iilkan aabachihchi.

Himaakaya Avak bitlihchi.

Choffihchi.

Haatanahlihchi.

Avak hoyaplihchi.

Chokoolihchi.

Pontokkoolin oompahchi.

Nochihchi.

English

Her name is Ava.

Ava lives on the reservation

Now, she is studying Alabama

Mikko is teaching her Alabama

Now, she is dancing.

She is jumping up and down.

She is spinning around

Ava is tired.

She is sitting down.

She is eating lunch.

She is sleeping.

Hover over me to reveal!

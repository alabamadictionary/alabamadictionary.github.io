About

Alabama Hour is a series of online lessons taught on Zoom designed to teach the Alabama language to learners of all levels. Lessons are one-hour long each, and are designed to be accessible to learners of all ages and backgrounds. While learners do not have to attend each session, since material builds on what comes before it, it is recommended that learners attend each session or self-study the materials provided on this website in order to get the most out of the program.

The lessons are organized into a series of modules, each focusing on a specific aspect of the language. Each module includes a variety of exercises and activities designed to help learners practice their skills and reinforce their understanding of the material.

No Cost to Attend!

There is no cost to attend any lessons for any learners who are interested, and all materials are provided free of charge.
